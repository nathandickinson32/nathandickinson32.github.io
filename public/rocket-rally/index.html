    Supplies, team colors & recovery

    Players 1–2 use teal track; players 3–4 use coral track. Your car can use only your team’s colored track. Cars pass through opposing team structures. Cream star platforms are shared.

    Crates refill every 14 seconds. Roads and barriers cost 10 materials, ramps 15, launch pads 20. Removing your own nearby piece refunds its cost, up to 200 stored. A shortage? The driver can press R and wait at the last earned crate. Recovery keeps checkpoints, cores, supplies and track.

    Free building & advanced driving

    Builder: 1–4 selects a piece; Q / E lowers or raises the floor; R rotates; right-click removes an owned nearby piece. Turn Guides Off for free building. WASD orbits in third person; scroll zooms. Hold F waits for range or supplies. Click the build button for one piece. T fixes the next wrong route piece: turning a backward ramp is free, upgrading a road to a ramp costs 5.

    Driver: hold Space for a higher jump, use WASD + Space to air dodge, Q / E to air roll, and X to drift. Braking takes priority over throttle and boost. Team signals last six seconds; Wait brakes an AI driver but never overrides a human’s controls.

    Playing with friends & connection help

    Create a room and copy its invite. Each person chooses a role and presses Ready; the host starts. AI fills unclaimed seats. The host chooses Easy, Standard or Hard before the race; changing it clears readiness. AI difficulty changes driving pace and building speed. The host also chooses the course; course changes clear readiness. Swap roles or add friends in the lobby between rounds.

    P pauses everyone. Only the host resumes, with a three-second countdown. Switching away from the host tab pauses the race. A guest disconnecting returns everyone to the lobby; the host leaving closes the room. Use ↔ to check latency or test the relay. Restrictive networks may prevent direct connections.

    Learn & practice has a tutorial and solo practice where Y swaps roles. Tab moves between controls; click the arena to use gameplay keys. H toggles the driver’s extra HUD details. Escape opens this help and pauses local practice. Online races keep running until someone pauses the room.
